# KAMPI - Game Rules

## 1. Game Setup
- **Players**: 2 to 6 players.
- **Deck**: Dynamic deck size based on player count (Players * 3 cards). Top ranks are used (A, K, Q, J...).
- **Starting Balance**: ₹100 per player.
- **Pot**: Money collected from bets is stored in the central pot.

## 2. Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)
1.  **Trail (Set)**: Three cards of the same rank (e.g., AAA, KKK).
2.  **Color (Flush)**: Three cards of the same suit (e.g., ♥♥♥).
3.  **Pair**: Two cards of the same rank (e.g., AAK).
4.  **High Card**: If no other hand is made, the highest card wins.

*(Note: Straights/Sequences are NOT valid hands in this version)*

## 3. Betting Rules
- **Blind**: You play without seeing your cards. You pay the base stake (₹1 or ₹2).
- **Seen**: You look at your cards. You must pay **2x** the current Blind stake.
- **Cross**: A Blind player can choose to "Cross" (become Seen) by paying the current stake. This forces you to play as "Seen" from then on. You can only Cross if you haven't seen your cards yet.
- **Blind Lockout**: Once ANY player becomes "Seen", no other player can place "Blind" bets. They must either "See My Cards" (and bet Seen amount) or "Cross".

## 4. Special Scenarios
### Three-Round Force Showdown
- If 3 or more players are active, and everyone is playing "Seen" for 3 consecutive rounds without raising the bet, a **Forced Showdown** occurs automatically to prevent endless loops.

### Heads-Up (2 Players Left)
When only two players remain, special rules apply:
1.  **No Force Showdown**: The 3-round limit is disabled.
2.  **Auto-Seen**: If your opponent is "Seen", you CANNOT playing Blind or Cross. You are automatically forced to "See" your cards.
3.  **Display Your Cards**: A special button appears. You can pay the current bet amount to immediately reveal the opponent's cards and declare a winner (Side Show / Showdown).
4.  **Distribute Pot**: Players can propose to split the pot 50/50. If both agree, the game ends and money is shared.

## 5. Winning
- The player with the best hand at a Showdown wins the entire pot.
- If everyone else folds, the last standing player wins the pot immediately.
- If the previous winning hand was a **TRAIL**, the stakes for the *next* game are doubled (Blind ₹2 / ₹4).
