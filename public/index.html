KAMPI Rules

1. Balance: Everyone starts with ₹100.

2. Betting:

  • Blind: Play without seeing cards. (₹1 or ₹2)
  • Seen: Play after seeing cards. Must bet 2x Blind amount.
  • Cross: Switch from Blind to Seen by matching current bet. (One time only).
  • Limits: If a player is SEEN, the Blind option is LOCKED for others.

3. Three-Round Limit: If 3 rounds pass with everyone SEEN and no drops, a Forced Showdown occurs.

4. Heads-Up (2 Players):

  • No force showdown. Game continues until explicit Showdown.
  • Display Cards: Pay current bet to force Showdown.
  • Auto-Seen: If opponent is Seen, you cannot play Blind/Cross. Your cards are auto-revealed.
